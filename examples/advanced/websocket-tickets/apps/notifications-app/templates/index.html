📚 How WebSocket Tickets Work & Why

  • Security First: Tickets are short-lived (10 seconds) and single-use to prevent replay attacks
  • No CSRF Cookies Needed: Unlike traditional WebSocket auth, tickets eliminate the need for CSRF tokens
  • Multi-App SSO: Works seamlessly across multiple apps in a single domain
  • Flow: JWT Cookie → Request Ticket → Connect WebSocket → Ticket Consumed

Why Tickets? WebSockets can't send custom headers, making CSRF protection tricky. Tickets solve this by creating a one-time authentication token that expires quickly, reducing the window for interception while maintaining security.
